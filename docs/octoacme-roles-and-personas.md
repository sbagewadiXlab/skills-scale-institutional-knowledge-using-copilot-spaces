# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Receive technical guidance and architectural decisions; participate in design reviews
- **With Product Managers**: Clarify requirements and acceptance criteria; provide estimates and technical constraints
- **With Product Specialist**: Understand product requirements and translate them into technical implementation
- **With Project Managers**: Report progress and risks; participate in sprint planning

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Specialist**: Collaborate on translating business vision into technical requirements
- **With Developers**: Provide clear requirements and acceptance criteria; discuss technical trade-offs
- **With Customer Success/Advocacy**: Gather customer feedback and validate product direction
- **With Project Managers**: Align on priorities and timeline expectations

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Track progress; identify and escalate blockers and risks
- **With Product Managers**: Align on scope, priorities, and timeline
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Coordinate on technical dependencies and architectural milestones
- **With Program Coordinator**: Delegate administrative tasks and meeting coordination
- **With Operations/Compliance**: Ensure compliance requirements are integrated into project plans

---

## Product Specialist

### Role Summary
Product Specialists bridge product vision with execution, translating business requirements into technical specifications and ensuring alignment between product goals and delivery. They serve as the connective link between product strategy and technical implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Translate business requirements and product vision into detailed technical specifications
- Conduct requirements analysis and document acceptance criteria
- Facilitate communication between product managers and developers
- Validate that implemented solutions align with product intent
- Maintain detailed feature documentation and user stories
- Identify and communicate gaps between product vision and technical constraints

### Decision-Making Authority
- Final say on requirements interpretation and acceptance criteria
- Authority to approve technical specifications against product requirements
- Can escalate conflicts between product intent and technical feasibility

### Goals
- Ensure products deliver intended business value
- Minimize rework due to misaligned requirements
- Bridge the gap between product and engineering perspectives
- Improve clarity and reduce ambiguity in feature implementation

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and specification reviews
- User story refinement sessions
- Feature walkthroughs and acceptance testing
- Documentation and specification maintenance

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Receive high-level business requirements; clarify product strategy and success metrics
- **With Developers**: Provide detailed specifications; answer clarifying questions during implementation; conduct acceptance testing
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Discuss technical feasibility and identify alternative approaches to meet requirements
- **With Project Managers**: Contribute to scoping and timeline estimation based on requirements complexity

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical decisions, ensure system design aligns with long-term vision, manage technical debt discussions, and mentor the development team. They provide technical direction and ensure architectural consistency across projects.

### Responsibilities
- Design and document system architecture and technical approach
- Make or guide key technical decisions and trade-offs
- Review technical proposals and architectural changes
- Identify and manage technical debt
- Mentor developers and support skill development
- Ensure alignment with organizational technical standards
- Validate that implementations follow architectural guidelines

### Decision-Making Authority
- Final authority on architectural decisions and technical approach
- Authority to approve or reject designs based on long-term technical strategy
- Can mandate technical standards and practices across the team

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable systems
- Reduce long-term technical debt
- Enable team growth through mentorship
- Balance short-term delivery with long-term quality

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decisions
- Code review guidance and mentorship
- Technical documentation and patterns
- Escalation of technical risks and challenges

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Developers**: Provide architectural guidance; review designs; mentor on technical practices
- **With Project Managers**: Communicate technical risks and dependencies; estimate technical effort
- **With Product Specialist**: Discuss technical feasibility of requirements; identify alternative solutions
- **With Operations/Compliance**: Ensure architectural decisions comply with operational and security requirements

---

## Operations/Compliance

### Role Summary
Operations and Compliance personnel ensure adherence to organizational policies, compliance requirements, and operational standards. They coordinate with stakeholders to ensure governance, security, and regulatory compliance throughout project delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define and enforce operational policies and procedures
- Ensure compliance with regulatory and organizational standards
- Manage security and data protection requirements
- Coordinate compliance reviews and audits
- Communicate operational constraints to project teams
- Monitor and report on compliance metrics and risks
- Escalate compliance issues and gaps

### Decision-Making Authority
- Final authority on compliance and regulatory requirements
- Authority to enforce operational standards and security policies
- Can halt or modify work that violates compliance requirements

### Goals
- Maintain compliance with all regulatory and organizational standards
- Minimize compliance-related risks and audit findings
- Enable smooth operations and audits
- Build trust through transparent governance

### Typical Communication
- Compliance requirements documentation
- Policy and procedure updates
- Compliance review meetings and audits
- Risk and issue escalation
- Training and awareness communication

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Integrate compliance requirements into project plans; identify compliance-related risks
- **With Developers**: Communicate security and operational standards; review implementation for compliance
- **With Technical Lead/Architect**: Ensure architectural decisions meet compliance and security requirements
- **With Product Managers**: Identify compliance constraints that impact product strategy

---

## Customer Success/Advocacy

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Advocacy professionals represent end-user and customer perspectives in project planning and delivery. They ensure customer needs are addressed, facilitate feedback loops, and advocate for customer-centric improvements.

### Responsibilities
- Represent customer voice and perspective in product decisions
- Gather and communicate customer feedback and pain points
- Validate that solutions address customer needs
- Facilitate user research and feedback sessions
- Ensure customer success metrics are tracked and addressed
- Advocate for customer-centric improvements
- Escalate critical customer issues and blockers

### Decision-Making Authority
- Authority to represent customer perspective in feature prioritization
- Can raise concerns if solutions don't address customer needs
- Influence on product direction based on customer feedback

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and retention
- Ensure customer voice is heard in product decisions
- Identify and address customer pain points
- Build strong customer relationships and advocacy

### Typical Communication
- Customer interviews and feedback sessions
- User research and testing sessions
- Customer issue reports and escalations
- Customer-centric metrics and dashboards
- Stakeholder updates on customer sentiment

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Product Managers**: Share customer feedback and insights; influence prioritization based on customer needs
- **With Product Specialist**: Validate requirements against customer needs; conduct user acceptance testing
- **With Developers**: Represent customer perspective during implementation; gather feedback on delivered features
- **With Project Managers**: Communicate customer expectations and satisfaction metrics

---

## Program Coordinator

### Role Summary
Program Coordinators manage the administrative aspects of program execution, coordinate communications between teams, and track milestones and deliverables. They ensure program logistics run smoothly and keep stakeholders informed.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate meeting scheduling and logistics
- Maintain project schedules, calendars, and tracking systems
- Compile and distribute status reports and communications
- Track action items, decisions, and commitments
- Maintain project documentation and repositories
- Support administrative preparation for key events (kickoffs, reviews, retrospectives)
- Assist in resource coordination and scheduling

### Goals
- Ensure smooth program operations and communications
- Reduce administrative burden on team members
- Maintain transparency and documentation
- Enable effective team collaboration

### Typical Communication
- Meeting coordination and reminders
- Status report compilation and distribution
- Calendar management and scheduling
- Documentation maintenance and updates
- Action item tracking and follow-up

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Support project coordination and meeting logistics; maintain documentation and tracking
- **With All Roles**: Coordinate schedules; gather status updates; facilitate communication across team

## Stakeholder Interaction Matrix

| Role | Primary Stakeholders | Key Dependencies |
|------|---------------------|------------------|
| **Developer** | Technical Lead, Product Manager, Product Specialist | Requires specifications, technical guidance, architectural decisions |
| **Product Manager** | Product Specialist, Customer Success, Stakeholders | Requires customer feedback, technical feasibility input, market insights |
| **Project Manager** | All roles | Requires progress updates, risks, and coordination across teams |
| **Product Specialist** | Product Manager, Developers, Technical Lead | Bridges business requirements with technical execution |
| **Technical Lead** | Developers, Operations, Architects | Sets technical direction and standards |
| **Operations/Compliance** | Project Managers, Technical Lead, Developers | Ensures governance and regulatory requirements |
| **Customer Success/Advocacy** | Product Manager, Product Specialist, Developers | Represents customer perspective and feedback |
| **Program Coordinator** | Project Manager, All roles | Supports logistics and communication |
